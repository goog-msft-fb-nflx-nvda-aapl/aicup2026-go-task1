# Full training configuration of the two best submissions (for the organizer)
Task 1 `id_v2_idftJ.csv` = website 0.8543 (idftI + query-graph smoothing, see 'Query graph'); idftI = 0.8528 (rank 11/90; idftH = same without c11/c12, 0.8518). Task 2 `rank_hsl7.csv` = website 0.7248 (rank 33/141). Commands: scripts/*.sh (21_task1_idft.sh, 32_task2_hsl7.sh). Verified settings: backup/results_ckpt_args.json (read from the checkpoints). Logs: backup/logs. All code in src/. No hidden labels or manual edits are used; data are the official training csv + test packages.

## Common
- Data: official AILAB-NDHU/aicup2026-go-dataset v1.1, 1,000,000 games -> data/train_arr.npz (moves as pos = x*19+y, 361 = pass; colour; handicap; rank label; player id). Hold-out for all validation: 800 players = `np.random.default_rng(1234).choice(np.unique(P), 800, replace=False)`.
- Augmentation: 8 board symmetries at training (random), 4 (idft) or 8 (rank) symmetries at test (TTA). bf16 autocast. Optimizer AdamW(wd 0.05, betas .9/.98), OneCycleLR (pct_start .03-.05), grad-clip 1.0. maxlen 320 moves.
- Move LM (`train_lm.py`): rank-conditioned causal transformer; input embeddings of position, colour, ply, handicap, target colour, rank condition; loss on target-colour moves. lm1 d384 L8 h6 ep4 bs128 lr4e-4; lm2 d512 L10 h8 ep8 bs128 lr3e-4; lm3 d768 L12 h12 ep8 bs96 lr2.5e-4; seed 0; condition id 10 = "unknown rank" (mean of the 10 rank embeddings) used by all downstream models.

## Task 1 (idftH)
Model (`train_idft.py`): pretrained LM (lm1/2/3) fine-tuned end-to-end; per game pooled hidden = concat(mean over own-colour moves, mean over all moves) -> Linear(2d,1024)-GELU-Linear(1024,256) -> L2-normalised embedding; ArcFace (scale s, additive angular margin m) over the 8,007 (or 7,207 hold-out-excluded) train players, class weights W (256-d); lr: LM `--lr`, head 1e-3; bs128, 7,004-7,812 steps/epoch.
Models (tag: init, epochs, lr, s, m, seed, players): a2 lm3 2 5e-5 20 .05 0 train-only | b1 lm3 4 1e-4 20 .1 0 | b2 lm2 4 5e-5 20 .05 1 | b3 lm1 4 5e-5 20 .05 2 | c1 lm3 6 1e-4 20 .1 5 all | c2 lm2 6 5e-5 20 .05 6 all | c3 lm3 6 5e-5 20 .05 7 all | c4 lm3 10 1e-4 20 .1 21 all | c5 lm2 8 1e-4 20 .1 22 all | c6 lm3 8 1e-4 24 .1 31 all | c7 lm1 8 1e-4 20 .1 41 all | c8 lm2 10 1.5e-4 20 .1 42 all | c9 lm2 12 2e-4 20 .1 61 all | c10 lm3 10 1.5e-4 20 .1 62 all | c11 lm2 10 1.5e-4 20 .1 91 all | c12 lm3 10 1e-4 20 .05 92 all (idftH used a2..c10; idftI adds c11 x2, c12 x1). (s=30/m=0.3 collapses.)
Inference (`idft_pools.py`): per pool, candidate prototype = L2-normalised mean embedding of its games; query = mean of its games; cosine score, 4-symmetry TTA.
Fusion (`ens_sidft.py`, `mk_gate4.py`): per-model row z-scored scores averaged with weights = repeat counts in `ens_sidft.py ens7 a2 b1 b1 c1 c1 c3 c3 c2 b2 b3 c4 c4 c5 c5 c6 c6 c7 c8 c8 c8 c9 c9 c10 c10 c10 c11 c11 c12` (ens6 = without the last three tokens); S = z(5-view score) + 3*z(idft ens) on the new half (+1*z on the original half); CSLS (top-3, a=0.6) on the new half; rank-consistency gate (log of posterior-product of fine-tuned rank models ft4/ft5, weight 2 new / 3 original, temperatures in mk_gate4.py); Sinkhorn (T=1.4) only on the original half. 5-view base = hashed own-move n-gram tf-idf SVD1000 MLP-ArcFace (6 seeds, EP6 H2048 margin .2 drop .3 noise .1) + frozen-LM-feature MLPs (lm1 x.5, lm2, lm3) + idf-cosine max-over-games x.25 (id_final6.py).

## Task 2 (hsl7)
Components (all combined as log-probabilities; weights chosen on the 800 hold-out players' bags, `blend2.py`): 0.5 x ft45 + 0.5 x HumanSL-LightGBM + 1 x mean(g1..g4) + 2 x mean(s1..s3); decision = argmax of expected score (exact 1, adjacent 1/e).
- ft45 (`train_ft.py`): ft4_sord8 = lm3 init, SORD tau 1.0, 8 epochs, lr 8e-5, pool both (own+all), bs128, seed 0; ft5_sord8 = tau 1.5, seed 1. Per-game log-probs summed over a player's games (8-symmetry TTA, rank_predict_ft.py).
- HumanSL features (`hsl_extract2.py`, EXTERNAL WEIGHTS KataGo b18c384nbt-humanv0.ckpt, KataGo v1.15.0 python code): 8 profiles 20k/10k/5k/2k/1d/3d/5d/8d (inverse-rank metadata 29/19/14/11/9/7/5/2, both players human, KGS source, 2020-03-01, byo-yomi 1200s+30s x5), first 100 moves per game; per move: log-prob of the played move, entropy and top-1 hit per profile, win-rate and score lead (profile 1d). Training games: 2,400 + 3,000 non-hold players x 25 games + 800 hold players x <=40 games (trainsel/trainsel2 selections in the script, seeds 7/11), plus all test games.
- HumanSL LightGBM (`hsl_rank3*.py`): per-game features (own/opponent mean log-prob per profile, differences, std, early/late halves, lead-loss statistics, handicap), bag features = mean+std+median over the bag; LGBMClassifier(multiclass, 600 trees, lr .03, 15 leaves, min_child 40, subsample .8, colsample .7); bags of 5/10/15/20 games, 12 per player and size.
- g1..g4 (`train_ftbag.py`, bag-level SORD fine-tune): g1 lm3 ep3 nmax12 np16 lr1e-4 seed0 | g2 lm2 ep4 seed1 | g3 lm3 ep4 seed2 nmax20 np12 tau1.5 | g4 lm2 ep5 seed3 nmax16 tau1.2 lr1.5e-4; 7,000 steps/epoch, each step = np players x n(5..nmax) games, mean of per-game pooled hidden -> head.
- s1..s3 (`train_hslseq.py`): transformer (3 layers, 4 heads, FFN 2d, dropout .1, learned positions) over the per-move HumanSL feature sequence (27 features + own-move flag + validity + handicap, perspective-corrected), pooled own/all, bag mean, SORD head; s1 d128 30 ep | s2 d192 80 ep seed1 | s3 d128 80 ep seed2 lr3e-4; 400 steps/epoch of 32 players x 5..12 games, AdamW lr 5e-4 (OneCycle).
Local checks / calibration: README.md, CALIBRATION.md (hold-out score .7176 -> website .7248).

## Task 1 query graph (idftJ, +0.0015 website)
`mk_gate5.py`: on the new pools only, after CSLS and before the rank gate: A = cosine between per-query embeddings (concatenation of the 16 models' unit query embeddings from `idft_qemb.py`), keep each query's top-3 neighbours with cosine > 0.5, symmetrise, row-normalise -> A_hat; S <- S + 0.5 * A_hat @ S. beta 0.3 not uploaded; beta 0.8 gave 0.8519 (worse). Rationale: the new pools repeat candidates (315 distinct top-1 among 400 queries vs ~292 expected with repeats, 400 without). Local simulator with repeated queries: 0.762 -> 0.785.
